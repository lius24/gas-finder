how the math works.

For each candidate station, the gross savings (price difference × tank size) is reduced by the drive cost (extra round-trip miles ÷ mpg × the candidate's own price). Tank and mpg default to 12 gal / 25 mpg; renders as a range to absorb the assumption. 

net_savings = (baseline_price − station_price) × tank
              − (extra_miles / mpg) × station_price
